# Temple skull — source and licence

"human skull 0" by **CDmir**, released under **CC0** (public domain dedication).
Page: https://opengameart.org/content/human-skull-0
File: `skull-fbx.zip` (9,685,693 bytes), downloaded 2026-10-18 with the user's approval.

`source/` keeps the model (`skull-Low4K.fbx`, 4,548 triangles) and the three maps used
(`Skull-Low.png` diffuse, `Skull-Low-normal.png`, `Skull-AO.png`, all as downloaded).
`Tools/build_temple_statues.py` scales it to real size, turns it to face +X, and ages and
reduces its texture to `../T_TempleSkull_D.png` / `../T_TempleSkull_N.png`.
