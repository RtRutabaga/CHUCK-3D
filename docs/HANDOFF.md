Verify-Package runs both audits on all three regions against baselines. The findings (loop-seam pops on the keeper and the ship's dancer, fencers' thumbs; temple sounds heard above ground; the smith's voice +5.5 dB) are in [AUTOMATION-DEFECT-CATALOG.md](AUTOMATION-DEFECT-CATALOG.md) and are not fixed yet.
