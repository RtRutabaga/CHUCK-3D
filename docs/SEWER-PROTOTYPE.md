# Winding sewer prototype

The user authorized sewer work on October 2. This supersedes earlier sewer-interior exclusions for this connected prototype, not the full campaign. Entry is a physical fall through the open rusty hatch beside the Dock Street side gate, opposite the bench. No interaction question, loading prompt or NPC dialogue.

The route is approximately 244 metres long, nine metres below the court. Positive world Y is north. From the landing it snakes north from Y3900 toward Y12300, crosses east, then bends repeatedly back south toward Y2200. It is one continuous curved passage, with a temporary collapsed terminus. No copied 2D map, enemies, quests or new campaign destination. A comparison to the exact 2D route length has not been measured.

The cross-section is a rounded arch approximately 3.0–3.4 metres wide and 3.2 metres high, broadening at the landing. Irregular mesh bulges and rounded stone instances give the walls and ceiling knobbly contours. Collision follows the continuous arch and floor; individual small surface stones are decorative. Eleven purple Astral Sea ruptures interrupt the central floor and drainage. Each is approximately 2.6 metres long and 1.1 metres wide, with stone banks alongside. Their visible nebula bed and animated translucent oil-slick films have no collision: stepping into a rupture falls and resets Chuck to the docks. This is procedural prototype art, not finished environment assets.

Warm sewer lamps are removed. Purple light from the ruptures is the underground illumination; outdoor sun/skylight are disabled while the local camera is underground and restored on return. Surface town lighting stays as authored. The astral visual wells extend three metres below the opening with closed sides and ends, preventing an angled camera from seeing the outdoor sky through the holes. All well faces remain noncolliding. Two thin, smoothly tessellated oil-slick films use animated interference colors and vertex warping; this remains an initial procedural effect.

The supplied `Sewer.wav` plays underground, crossfading over 1.25 seconds with the docks score. It loops its 174-second recording; a seamless musical join has not been established. Source provenance and SHA-256 are in SourceAssets/Audio/README.md.

The shaft and sewer occupy the same running world as the docks. The former blackout plane is removed and the harbor surface is cut around the shaft. A narrow `IsWithinDockSewer` exception lets Chuck fall down the shaft and walk the underground route without triggering the original below-quay reset. Falling outside that footprint still resets; R / controller View returns to the dock spawn. There is no climb-back exit or sewer checkpoint yet. Existing walk/run/jump/camera controls remain unchanged.

Implementation is in `DockSewer.cpp/.h`. It reuses the existing bundled ProceduralMeshComponent module and stone meshes/material; two new astral materials and the user-supplied audio are LFS assets. No installation, external download or original-game mutation. `-ChuckSewerCapture` records four fixed inside views. `-ChuckSewerTest` drops the actual character through the grate, checks underground landing, follows the whole route on the outer stone bank at bends using movement input, then deliberately falls into a rupture and checks reset and surface-light restoration. It also starts the score near its end to check looping. The standard packaged verifier traces/sweeps 372 safe-bank samples and verifies eleven genuine floor gaps. Fixed captures and automated checks do not replace physical-controller playtesting, listening or subjective camera feedback. See HANDOFF for actual run evidence and remaining defects.
