# High wall lanterns beside the large pier

The iron bracket lanterns sit on the opposed side walls of the four tall pitched-roof rear-row Dock Street houses, between the sewer entrance and the large northern pier. This is the rear row centred at Y2830, not the timber workshops by the starting wharf or the small dock by the tavern. The earlier workshop placements were removed before launcher promotion after the user clarified the location.

**October 9 brachiation revision (Claude):** the original twelve lanterns (two per wall) were replaced with a brachiation run. Each of the three 120 cm alleys has five lanterns that alternate walls along it, at y 2570, 2705, 2830, 2955 and 3090, clear of the shuttered windows at y 2644/3016. Arm tops are at 612/622/616/626/612 cm, beneath every eave. The mounting plates are now flush on the plaster; the earlier mounts stood about 14 cm proud. Each cage hangs a forged lamplighter's ring: an eye, a link, and a 7 cm ring across the alley with a worn-bright bottom bar. That bar is the grip, at 543–557 cm. The dressing is the same as before: dark iron plates and rivets, diagonal and curled braces, open cages, rain caps and vent necks, warm animated flames, and the existing flicker. Existing materials only. The lanterns have no collision.

House numbers 1–4 run west to east (centres X -1450, -770, -90, 590). The lane between houses n and n+1 has walls at X = c(n)+280 and c(n)+400. Lanterns at index 0, 2 and 4 are on the western wall (`WallLampGrip_Rear{n}_East_{i}`), and index 1 and 3 on the eastern wall (`WallLampGrip_Rear{n+1}_West_{i}`). Each sits 32 cm out from its wall. The named, `DockLampGrip`-tagged scene components remain. The gameplay contract is `AddChuckSwingGrip` / `GetChuckSwingGrips` in `ChuckClimbable.h`: grip point plus the alley's line, registered five per lane, west lane first.
