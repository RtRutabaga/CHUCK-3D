# Prototype-era archive

These documents record the Waterdeep prototype, from 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-12. They are kept for evidence and design detail, not as current instructions. Where they conflict with [../PROJECT-BRIEF.md](../PROJECT-BRIEF.md), [../../AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) or a region plan, those win. That includes every "prototype only / do not expand into the campaign" statement.

- `HANDOFF-PROTOTYPE.md`: the full handoff log until 2026-10-12, newest first.
- `agent-handoffs/`: the Claude and Codex per-session logs from the same period.
- `PROJECT-BRIEF-PROTOTYPE.md` and `AGENT-WORKFLOW-HISTORY.md`: the earlier brief and the v1/v2 workflows.
- `PROTOTYPE.md` and `PLAYTEST.md`: the camera-comparison milestone and the accumulated playtest notes. Current controls are in [../PLAYING.md](../PLAYING.md).
- **Per-feature notes for Waterdeep** (summarised in [../regions/WATERDEEP.md](../regions/WATERDEEP.md)):
  - Setting and buildings: DOCKS-SETTING, WATERDEEP-PLAZA, COASTAL-VISTA, WEATHERED-TIMBER, TRADE-SIGNS, WALL-LAMPS
  - Sewer: SEWER-PROTOTYPE, SEWER-WALLRIFT, SEWER-SPRINT-RIFT
  - Tavern and the return state: TAVERN-INTERIOR, TAVERN-PANTRY, DOCKS-RETURN
  - Effects and presentation: FORGE-DRESSING, FIRE-PASS, SURFACE-WATER, GRAPHICS-PASS, TUTORIALS, SUBTITLES
- `DEMO-RELEASE.md`: the October 9 friends demo. Its package inputs `DEMO-README.txt` and `DEMO-CREDITS.txt` stay in `docs/` for `Tools/Package-FriendsDemo.ps1`.
- `CHUCK-V1-INTEGRATION.md`, `AI-DEV-NOTES-REVIEW.md`, `RESOURCE-GUIDE-NOTES.md`, `agent-tasks/`: one-off integration notes and early task files.
